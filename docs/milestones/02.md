## What I expected, what happened, what I learned

**Expected:** the DP would start far less often than the price-taker's 14 times, and the
margin would fall below the baseline by roughly the no-load bill (about $1.5M) plus a
few starts.

**Happened:** 4 starts against 14. The DP is $1,798,626.94 below the baseline: no-load is
$1,542,240.00 of that, starts $166,235.00, and the remaining $90,151.94 is energy margin
given up by running through weak hours or sitting out thin ones. Against the feasible
price-taker the DP is $369,170.81 better (2.3%), from 6 fewer starts and 40 fewer hours
on line.

**Learned:**

1. **No-load matters more than starts in a strong month.** At $7.59/MMBtu, 400 MMBtu/h
   costs about $3,000 an hour, every hour on line; four starts cost $166k in total.
2. **The baseline overstates margin by about 10%** once the costs of being on line are
   charged, before any outage or real gas basis.
3. **Uplift only appears for the naive solver.** The DP never commits to a block that
   loses money, so it earns none; the price-taker with limits earns $400k of make-whole,
   which is more than the DP's whole advantage over it. If uplift were counted as
   margin, the naive solver would look as good as the optimal one. It is reported on its
   own line and kept out of the energy margin for that reason (Q-030).
4. **Invariant 3's tolerance had quietly widened.** With four fuel-side lines in a start
   hour the old check allowed two cents. It now compares the sum of each component
   rounded to the cent, exactly.
5. **A test of mine was wrong, not the solver.** I expected the DP to stay on overnight
   through eight weak hours; it shut down and restarted hot, which is $6,720 cheaper.

## Changed from milestone 1

* `Offer` carries the three-part fields; `PlantState` carries the running cost and DA
  revenue of the current commitment block; `RunMeta` carries the solver and the solver
  comparison; `RunResult` carries the per-day bounds and each comparison solver's output.
* `commitment.solve` now takes the planning horizon (a list of day prices and offers).
* New rules: `docs/methodology.md` §13. New questions: Q-030 to Q-034.

## UI view touched

Flow screen: a commitment timeline under the price plot, one track per solver with starts
marked and each solver's starts, hours on and margin; a start-cost arrow from the plant
to the ledger that is drawn only in the hour of a start; the ledger node shows start and
no-load cost and uplift; the header names what the loaded run models; stored runs can be
switched from the run strip. Screenshots: `docs/screens/02-flow-start.png`,
`02-flow-light.png`, `02-flow-phone.png`.
