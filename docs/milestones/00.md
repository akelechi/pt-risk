## What I expected, what happened, what I learned

**Expected:** 8,760 hours per location with exactly one 23-hour and one 25-hour day; DA and
RT tables on identical hours; the derived energy component equal across locations.

**Happened:** all three, with two things that needed a decision.

1. **Fall-back hour has no marker.** NYISO prints `01:00` twice on 2025-11-02 with nothing
   to tell them apart. The loader reads file order (first = EDT, second = EST), and refuses
   a third occurrence, a missing second occurrence (the day would have 24 hours, not 25) or
   a repeat of any ordinary hour. Tests cover each case on verbatim NYISO rows.
2. **Energy component is not published, and is only equal across locations to 3 cents.**
   `energy = lbmp - loss + congestion` gives one system price per hour across all 15 zones
   on congested days; the opposite sign is off by more than $10/MWh. Because each published
   term is rounded to the cent, the recovered energy price differs between two locations by
   up to $0.02 in the data (bound $0.03). I first set the tolerance at $0.011 and the full
   year failed; the config now carries $0.031 with the derivation.

**Learned (things that change later milestones):**

* **The two Roseton units have identical LBMPs in every hour of 2025** (max difference
  $0.00, DA). They sit on one bus for pricing, so unit-level differences will come only
  from state and parameters, never from price.
* **EIA disagrees with the brief's placeholder parameters.** EIA-860 (2024): nameplate
  621 MW per unit, net summer capacity 614.8 / 607.7 MW, minimum load 140 MW, both units
  1974, primary fuel NG, secondary RFO. EIA-923 (2024): 2,763,532 MMBtu burned for
  236,658 MWh net, an annual average heat rate of **11.68 MMBtu/MWh** (brief: "~10.5"),
  at roughly a 2% capacity factor. I left the brief's values in the config as `assumed`
  and raised Q-018 rather than change them silently.
* **RT prices go negative and above $2,000/MWh** at this bus in 2025. Milestone 3 will
  need to show those hours explicitly; they will dominate the RT balancing line.
* **Henry Hub history has a dated row with no price** (2018-01-05). Outside the window, so
  it is ignored; inside the window the loader refuses rather than drops (Q-022).

## UI view touched

unit; the only live value is the assumed-parameter count in the header (33 of 36), read

## Not done in this milestone, by design

* No dispatch, no settlement, no ledger entries, no plant states.
* `GET /runs/{id}/hour/{ts}` (flow payload) and `GET /runs/{a}/bridge/{b}` are not served.
* No scrubber, no clickable arrows, no other screens.
* Oil, capacity, RGGI, weather and forward-curve tables are not loaded.
